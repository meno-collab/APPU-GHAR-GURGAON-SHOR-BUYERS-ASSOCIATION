About Us

A common platform for affected shop buyers

Appu Ghar Gurgaon Shop Buyers Association represents and coordinates the interests of shop buyers and allottees associated with the Appu Ghar Gurgaon project.

The Association aims to improve communication among members, share verified updates, maintain important records and support collective representation in matters affecting buyers.

Public court records identify the Association in proceedings concerning International Recreation and Amusement Limited (IRAL).
